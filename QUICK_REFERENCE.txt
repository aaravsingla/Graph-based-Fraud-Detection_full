================================================================================
🚨 COMMON ERRORS & FIXES
================================================================================

❌ NoSuchBucket
   Fix: grep S3_OUTPUT_BUCKET .env
   Should show: dev-login-graph-data

❌ AccessDenied
   Fix: Check AWS credentials in .env
   Should show AWS_ACCESS_KEY_ID and AWS_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY

❌ InvalidToken
   Fix: Session token expired, get new credentials
   Update: AWS_SESSION_TOKEN in .env

❌ Connection timeout
   Fix: Check internet, verify AWS_REGION=eu-west-1

❌ Export step failed (SQL/DynamoDB/OpenSearch)
   Fix: tail -50 export.log
